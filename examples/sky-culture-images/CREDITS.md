# Sky pictures — credits

These pictures of the 92 bright stars and the 88 constellations ship with Astro
Portal so that the target catalogue shows them without any import. They are
renders of the application's own sky map, made once and kept here:

- a bright star (`star-hip-<Hipparcos number>.jpg`, and `-full.jpg` for the
  full-screen viewer): centred on the star, ringed in gold, the lines of its
  constellation in full and its neighbours' paler;
- a constellation (`constellation-<IAU abbreviation>.jpg`, `-full.jpg`): its
  lines and its drawing, whole; `-naked.jpg` and `-naked-lines.jpg` are its
  bare sky in the same frame, the stars alone and with its lines, for the
  « Preview » of the target's sheet.

**They are made from Stellarium's material, not only from Astro Portal's
code:**

- the constellation drawings are by **Johan Meuris** for Stellarium, under the
  [Free Art License](https://artlibre.org/licence/lal/en/) — credited in the
  application as « Constellation drawings: Johan Meuris (Stellarium), Free Art
  License »;
- the constellation lines, boundaries and the Western sky culture's data are
  Stellarium's, under CC BY-SA;
- the stars come from the Hipparcos and Tycho catalogues (ESA).

Sizes: the card's picture is 360 × 360 pixels, the full-screen one 900 ×
900, the bare sky 696 × 696; JPEG. `sky-culture-images.json` lists every
file with its size in bytes.
